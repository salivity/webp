VP8 Intra-Prediction in WebP Lossy Files

WebP lossy compression relies directly on the VP8 video codec’s intra-prediction framework to compress static image data. Instead of encoding raw pixel values, VP8 splits an image into macroblocks and predicts their contents by extrapolating pixels from previously decoded adjacent blocks situated above and to the left. The encoder then only stores the prediction mode chosen and the residual difference between the prediction and the actual image data, drastically reducing file size.

Macroblock Partitioning

VP8 processes an image by dividing it into 16x16 pixel macroblocks. The prediction process operates on two primary components: luma (brightness, Y) and chroma (color, U and V).

To achieve optimal prediction fidelity across different image textures:

  • Luma (Y): Can be predicted as a single 16x16 block for smooth, uniform areas, or partitioned into sixteen 4x4 sub-blocks for detailed regions with edges or complex patterns.
  • Chroma (U and V): Subsampled to 8x8 blocks (4:2:0 format) and predicted as whole 8x8 units.

Prediction is strictly causal, meaning it only uses reconstructed boundary pixels from neighboring blocks located immediately above, to the left, or above-left of the target block.

4x4 Luma Prediction Modes

When a 16x16 macroblock contains fine detail, it is broken down into sixteen 4x4 sub-blocks. For each 4x4 sub-block, the encoder chooses one of ten prediction modes:

  1. DC Prediction (B_DC_PRED): Fills the 4x4 block with the mathematical average of the adjacent pixels from the top row and left column.
  2. Vertical Prediction (B_VE_PRED): Copies the four pixels directly above the block vertically downward across all rows.
  3. Horizontal Prediction (B_HE_PRED): Copies the four pixels directly to the left of the block horizontally across all columns.
  4. TrueMotion Prediction (B_TM_PRED): Extrapolates pixels using the formula Predicted = Left + Top - TopLeft, estimating directional color gradients.
  5. Directional Modes (6 Modes): VP8 includes six angled directional modes to trace distinct diagonal edges without creating step-like artifacts:
    • Left-Down (B_LD_PRED): Predicts pixels down-left along roughly a 45-degree angle.
    • Right-Down (B_RD_PRED): Predicts pixels down-right along roughly a 45-degree angle.
    • Vertical-Right (B_VR_PRED): Interpolates diagonally downward to the right at roughly a 27-degree angle from the vertical.
    • Vertical-Left (B_VL_PRED): Interpolates diagonally downward to the left at roughly a 27-degree angle from the vertical.
    • Horizontal-Down (B_HD_PRED): Interpolates diagonally downward to the right at roughly a 27-degree angle from the horizontal.
    • Horizontal-Up (B_HU_PRED): Interpolates diagonally upward to the right at roughly a 27-degree angle from the horizontal.

16x16 Luma and 8x8 Chroma Prediction Modes

For smoother, less detailed macroblocks, evaluating pixels at the 4x4 level is unnecessary. In these regions, 16x16 luma blocks and 8x8 chroma blocks utilize four shared prediction modes:

  • DC Prediction: The average of all available neighboring boundary pixels (top row and left column) fills the entire block.
  • Vertical Prediction: Extends the top boundary row downward through all rows.
  • Horizontal Prediction: Extends the left boundary column rightward through all columns.
  • TrueMotion Prediction: Extrapolates two-dimensional gradients across the entire 16x16 or 8x8 space based on the top, left, and top-left boundary samples.

Generating and Encoding the Residual

Once the encoder identifies the optimal prediction mode via rate-distortion optimization (RDO), it subtracts the predicted pixel values from the original pixel values. This leaves a residual matrix representing the error.

The residual is then transformed using a Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT)—or a Walsh-Hadamard Transform (WHT) for DC components—quantized according to the target quality level, and encoded using VP8's boolean arithmetic entropy coder. The decoder follows the reverse sequence: it reconstructs the prediction block based on the signaling mode and adds the decompressed residual to reproduce the final image.
